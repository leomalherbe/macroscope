# Use of artificial intelligence

*[Version française](AI-USE.fr.md)*

Macroscope is designed and maintained by Léo Malherbe. Its development relies heavily on an artificial-intelligence assistant: Claude, by Anthropic, used in Claude Code.

**Models used**
- Claude Fable 5.1: from 2 to 7 November 2026.
- Claude Opus 5.5: since 7 November 2026.

**What the AI did, on my instructions**
- Writing the code: the OPEN-MU economic engine, the pages (desk, board, console, model page), the Google Apps Script backend and the automated tests.
- The technical documentation (model specification, README) and the French translation of the interface.
- Design proposals (game rules, options, visualisations), which I accepted, changed or set aside.

**What is mine**
- The teaching design of the game, and the choice of the model and of its rules.
- The decisions: every change goes through a pull request that I read and merge myself.
- The trials in class and the corrections they led to.

**Traceability.** In the history of the repository, the AI's contributions are signed « Co-Authored-By: Claude … » with the name of the model.

**There is no AI during the game.** The results are computed by the economic model, deterministically; no game data is sent to an AI service.

**Responsibility.** Any error that remains, in the code, the model, its parameters or the texts, is my responsibility alone.

*Léo Malherbe, 14 November 2026*
